The flag photograph is released under CC0, so it requires no attribution and does not
restrict commercial use. The footer credits it anyway as a courtesy.

`eagle-fly.webp` is a 14-frame sprite strip built from the owner's own contact sheet:
each of the 9 cells was cut out, the black background segmented away (threshold, close,
hole-fill, 1px feather — a luminance ramp does not work here, it eats the dark wings),
the frames aligned on the beak, and one cell dropped because it was a side-on glide at a
different scale. The site steps two stacked copies through the strip one frame apart and
cross-fades between them, so the wing-beat dissolves rather than snaps.

The flag was cropped to its fabric — a derivative work, which CC0 allows.
