How a page works

The same thing you see on the home page, with your name on it.

1. Ask for a pagePick your address, say who you are and where you post. Each page is looked at before it goes public.
2. Send people to itPut your TorchHive link in your video descriptions and bios. Viewers follow you there to be told when bidding opens.
3. They bid, you do itThe highest bid sets the job, you do it on camera, and you are paid. TorchHive keeps 5% of each winning bid.
