Where it came from

It started as
a request.

There is a moment every parent knows. You are pulling out of the driveway, two small people are buckled in behind you, and one of them decides the current speed is a personal insult.

DaddyGoFaster is that moment, kept. It is not a team or a series or a sponsorship deal. It is a name — the one that got yelled first, then laughed about, then printed on a shirt, and now sits on the front of a channel, a shop, and this page.

If you have ever been the one driving, or the one yelling, you already get it.

Two kids. One gas pedal. One very American request.
