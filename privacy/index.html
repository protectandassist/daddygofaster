Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

This page says what TorchHive keeps about you, why, and how to have it removed. We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

If you make an account

  • Your email address. It is how you sign in and where alerts go. It is never shown to other visitors.
  • Your username. You pick it. It is shown beside your comments.
  • No password. You sign in with a link we email you, or with Google.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and its ID number for your account. We ask Google for nothing else, and we cannot see your Google password, contacts, or files.

If you bid or add money to a job

  • The name you put on the board and the amount are public.
  • The job you describe stays private until the video, unless you chose to make it an open job. Open jobs are public.
  • Your email address is kept so we can reach you about your bid. It is never shown.
  • Your card goes straight from your browser to Stripe, the card processor. We never see or store the card number. We keep Stripe's reference for the hold or charge.

If you turn on alerts

  • Email alerts go to your account's email address. Every alert has a link that turns them off.
  • Notifications on a device work through your browser's own notification service. We keep the address that service gives us for your device, and nothing else about the device. Turning notifications off, or signing out, removes it.

When you visit

  • We count visits ourselves: which page, roughly where from (city, from your network address), what kind of device, and which buttons were pressed. This uses no cookies and no advertising trackers.
  • Your network address is kept for 30 days, to tell visits apart and to spot abuse. Visit counts are kept for about 13 months.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, also counts page views for us without cookies.
  • Your browser keeps a few things on your own device so the site remembers you: that you are signed in, the name and email you last typed into the bid form, a private handle for each bid you placed, and your sound setting.

Who else handles your information

Only the services that make the site work, and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare, which runs the site and stores its data
  • Stripe, for card holds and payments
  • Resend, which sends our emails
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google
  • Your browser's notification service (run by Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), if you turn on notifications

Seeing, changing or removing your information

You can change your username and alert settings in your account. To see what we hold about you, or to have your account and information deleted, email hello@torchhive.com from the address on your account. Records of payments are kept as long as tax and card rules require.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to hello@torchhive.com.
